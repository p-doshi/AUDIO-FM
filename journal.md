## 2026-08-09 — paradigm correction: music2vec is data2vec-family, not JEPA

- **Decision reversed.** music2vec has been labeled "JEPA-family" since the very first CLAUDE.md draft and carried that framing through every turn of Phase 1 analysis so far, including treating its cross-model RSA isolation as informative about H1 ("JEPA-family models agree with each other more than with others"). User caught this and walked through the actual architectural distinction: data2vec-style models (music2vec) have a student encoder that operates directly on masked input and predicts the EMA teacher's averaged top-K layer representations — no separate predictor network. JEPA as specified (and as A-JEPA/Audio-JEPA's own methods sections describe it) has three components: context encoder, EMA-updated target encoder, and a *separate predictor network* conditioned on the context representation. Both lineages descend from BYOL and are self-distillation-with-EMA-teacher, but the decoupled predictor is the real dividing line, and music2vec doesn't have one.
- **Practical consequence**: as of this correction there are zero working JEPA-family models in the active comparison. `audio_jepa` (the `ltuncay/Audio-JEPA` substitute, still deferred pending its Hydra-config loader work) will be the *first* JEPA-family data point once wired up, not a second one to compare against music2vec — H1 as originally phrased (mutual agreement *among* JEPA-family models) needs two working checkpoints and isn't testable yet.
- **Corrected everywhere the mislabel appeared**: `CLAUDE.md`'s candidate-model table and H1 statement (both annotated in place with correction notes, not silently rewritten), `audio_comp/models/music2vec.py`'s `ModelInfo.paradigm` field and docstring, `README.md`'s model table. Did NOT rewrite earlier journal entries above that say "JEPA-family" for music2vec — journal is append-only per CLAUDE.md's own convention; this entry is the correction of record.
- **New confound documented for future work**: `ltuncay/Audio-JEPA` is trained on meaningfully less compute than the other active teachers (100k steps, ~14h on 4 V100s, 5,338h AudioSet vs. wav2vec2/data2vec's 400k steps on larger batches), and its own paper reports it substantially underperforming both baselines on several linear-probe tasks (e.g. Speech Commands V1: 0.152 vs. data2vec's 0.927). If it comes back RSA-isolated once wired up, that's confounded between "JEPA-paradigm geometry is genuinely different" and "this checkpoint is comparatively undertrained" — RSA alone can't tell them apart. Documented directly in `audio_comp/models/audio_jepa.py`'s docstring so it's visible right where the loader gets implemented.
- **A specific, testable prediction for later**: the Audio-JEPA paper states its objective favors embedding cohesion over linear separability (strong kNN, weak linear probe on the same tasks) — predicting a *low* TwoNN intrinsic dimension and/or unusually tight within-category clustering once run through `inspect_geometry.py`. This is the opposite direction from music2vec, which had the *highest* ID of the six active models. A low-ID/high-cohesion result for `audio_jepa` would be real, paper-grounded evidence for paradigm-driven geometry differences — meaningfully stronger than an unexplained isolated RSA number. Not run yet — `audio_jepa` still needs its loader wired up first.
